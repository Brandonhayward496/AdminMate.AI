About this build

AdminMate AI demo build — 7 modules: AI assistant, email and letter writer, customer management, quote and invoice generation, tasks and reminders, business dashboard, document storage and activity summaries. Drafts are produced by a local template engine, so the app works with no API key and no data leaves your device. The generation layer is isolated in aiGenerate() if you later want to swap in a live model.
